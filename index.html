About Me

I'm a Computer Science graduate from the University of Strathclyde, graduating with a First Class Honours degree. Since August 2025 I've been working as a Software Engineer, building and shipping production web applications, including developing a full production website from the ground up.

I have hands-on experience across the full stack: React and Vue.js on the frontend, Python on the backend, PostgreSQL for data, and running production jobs and services. I'm always looking to learn new technologies and continue growing as an engineer.
